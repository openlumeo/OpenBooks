# Concept A, "The Twin"

**Idea.** One ledger instrument is the proof: the released statement, with its lavender RELEASED stamp and an equation that ties. One switch alters a single digit (TXN 4 balance 436.46 to 436.47) and the same instrument turns into its refused twin, so the visitor sees in one glance that changing one digit makes it refuse.

**What the visitor sees and does.** Calm column left: H1 (2 lines), lede (2 lines), `Open workspace` plus a `Read the docs` link. Right: the instrument (opening, five real rows, closing, equation) with a slim one-line stage rail and the stamp, floating over a soft mint bloom and a faint plum atmosphere bottom right. Below it, one switch `ALTER ONE BALANCE`. Toggling it: the TXN 4 balance digit rolls mechanically, the Balance node turns rose with FAIL, TXN 4 and the closing row fade to rose borders, the RELEASED stamp lifts away and the rose `REFUSED · BALANCE_MISMATCH` lands, and the equation becomes `741.54 − 305.08 = 436.46 ≠ 436.47` with the verbatim engine message beneath. Toggling back restores it.

**Motion timeline (once, on load, about 2.3s).** 0.1s copy fades up 14px. 0.2s instrument rises 46px with 9deg X tilt to flat (1.5s, Lumeo ease). 0.75s rail nodes in. 0.85s rows tie in sequence (0.12s stagger) with their left bars drawing top to bottom. 1.5s equation fades in. 1.6s stamp lands (scale 1.32 to 1, rotate -6 to -2). The only other motion is the toggle: reel roll 640ms, border and stamp crossfades about 500ms. Optional pointer tilt, max 2.2deg, fine pointers only.

**Interaction and keyboard.** `<button role="switch" aria-checked>`, reachable by Tab, toggled by Space or Enter. Focus is the 2px accent outline at 3px offset. A polite live region announces "Refused. BALANCE_MISMATCH" or "Released". The sr-only balance text updates with the state. Nothing else is focusable apart from the nav and CTAs.

**Degradation.** No JS: the instrument renders in its final released frame and the switch is hidden (`.no-js`). Reduced motion: the entrance is skipped, parallax is off, and every transition collapses to 0.001ms. The static frame is the default state. Light theme flips via `data-theme="light"` on `<html>` (the theme button in the nav); the plum atmosphere is hidden there, as the tokens specify.

**Deviations, deliberate.** H1 is 40 to 60px (`clamp(40px, 4.05vw, 60px)`), below the Lumeo H1 scale, because the scale's 80px cannot hold two lines beside the instrument. Rows: the closing row is included in the fade ("rows below" the break). The lede is trimmed to fit two lines (`with the source of every figure` kept, the rest of the original trimmed). No claims were added.

**Building it in the real site.** Put the instrument in `web/components/landing/proof-pair.tsx` (it already renders from `proof-data.ts`). Keep `ReconPanel`/`ReconRow` as the row and bar primitives, and add the TXN 4 reel as a small client component. The switch becomes a `"use client"` island with `useState`. The entrance goes in `web/components/motion/` using the existing GSAP setup in `gsap-setup.ts` and `reveal.tsx`, behind `use-reduced-motion.ts`. Pointer tilt reuses `use-pointer-tilt.ts`. The nav is `landing-nav.tsx` unchanged. Replace the mock Balance state with a real prop from the engine result.
